# Future ideas

Not built yet. Kept here so they aren't lost.

## Captions

- **Show how long each word is spoken.** In the transcript, show each word's
  length (as the lecturer actually says it), for example as a thin bar under
  the word or on hover, so long, drawn-out words are easy to spot.
- **Max length per phrase.** Set a maximum duration (and/or words/characters)
  for each caption phrase, globally or per short. Phrases that run longer are
  split automatically.
- **Arrange phrases by hand.** Let the user move words between phrases, merge
  or split phrases, and nudge a phrase's start/end, directly on the timeline
  or in the transcript.

## Audio

- **Even out the volume on export.** Optional loudness normalisation (about
  −14 LUFS, a common target for social video) so quiet and loud lectures come
  out at the same level.
